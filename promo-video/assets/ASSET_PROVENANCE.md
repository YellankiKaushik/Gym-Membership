# Asset register

The audio files were synthesized specifically for this edit by `scripts/audio.mjs`. They contain no external recordings or sampled compositions. Music and effects are separate original stereo PCM stems. The project owner may use the generated score and effects with the video.

The PNGs in `captures/` are fresh local captures of the existing FitZone React components and CSS. All names, IDs, dates, and counts are illustrative fixtures; contacts are hidden, and no real backend is contacted. See the capture audit. No screenshot from docs/ui-review is used as a final asset.

The dumbbell path is the existing Lucide application's icon. Its ISC/MIT attribution is in LICENSE-lucide.txt. All remaining vector graphics and backgrounds are constructed in the video source. The film references Google Sheets solely to identify the verified integration.

Bahnschrift and Segoe UI are installed Windows system fonts used for local rendering. No font files are redistributed in this project. Existing gym photographs were inspected and deliberately excluded because their source resolution is small and redistribution provenance is not established. The unused full-page `captures/mobile-lookup.png` contains those photographs, is ignored, and remains local. No external photographic or video stock asset is used in the film or published captures.
