## Design language

Deep forest `#061b13`; emerald `#08745b`; soft mint `#a5f4bb`; ivory `#eaf2e9`; amber for genuine expiry states. Bahnschrift supplies controlled, athletic display typography; the application's Segoe UI remains in its captured interface. These are locally installed system fonts and are not redistributed.

The native Lucide dumbbell geometry forms the emblem. Fine, large circular outlines suggest a lens and training rhythm, with no particle field. A restrained luminous wash provides separation. White interface surfaces remain white, preserving recognition of the original product. Headlines occupy deliberate negative space and do not cover the key UI.

## Audio

The original `scripts/audio.mjs` synthesizes an E-minor electronic instrumental at 120 BPM with pads, arpeggios, bass, kick, restrained percussion, and a resolved tonic. Separate synthesized effects include soft interface ticks, transition air, and tonal impacts. No stock track, song, sample pack, or synthetic narrator is used. Music and effects are independently rendered to stereo 48 kHz WAV stems. The final mix targets -17 LUFS and -1.5 dBTP; the verification report records measured output. A future human voiceover is mixed independently, with the music bed reduced.
