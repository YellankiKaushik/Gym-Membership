## Cohesion and truth

Retain FitZone's native dumbbell emblem, forest/emerald/mint palette, ivory UI, and amber expiry status. Remove chapter numbering, repeated background rings, broad color washes, most explanatory microcopy, and unrelated pointer gestures from the hero. Product headings use a less condensed hierarchy; the main wordmark keeps the established athletic typography. A single fine mint rule connects the brand, input focus, summary inspection, records and final CTA.

Only existing functionality is shown: numeric lookup; loaded member summary; add/edit/renew forms; search/filter; expiry attention; Apps Script/Google Sheets; responsive UI. Synthetic fixture data and hidden contacts are retained. No production connection, automatic notification, live sync, payment, attendance, or multi-tenant feature is implied.

The existing 30-second vertical cutdown is preserved as its own earlier edit; this revision targets the requested 60-second hero. The clean and silent hero exports are refreshed with the revised picture. Music remains independently editable; interface effects are adjusted to the new action points. Script and SRT retain their feature windows.
